# 5 mortgage myths: Instagram carousel

`slide-1.png` to `slide-7.png` are 1080x1350 (Instagram 4:5 portrait). Upload them in order as one carousel post.

1. Cover: "5 mortgage myths that could cost you."
2. Myth #1: You need 20% down.
3. Myth #2: Your bank will give you the best rate.
4. Myth #3: Pre-approved means you're approved.
5. Myth #4: The lowest rate is always the best mortgage.
6. Myth #5: Just sign your renewal letter.
7. Call to action and disclaimer (general information, not financial advice).

Navy background, gold accents, Inter font (SIL Open Font License). No third-party images.

## Rebuild
Edit the text in `edit/build.py` (`MYTHS`, `cover()`, `cta()`), then run `python3 edit/build.py`.
